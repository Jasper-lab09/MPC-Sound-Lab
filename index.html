Where sound becomes data,

MPC Sound Lab is a browser-based platform for exploring the intersection of physics, mathematics, and audio engineering. Analyze live sound, generate music from mathematical structures, and experience audio through visual translation.

§ 01 Concept
Premise
Sound is physical — pressure waves propagating through air at 343 m/s. It is also mathematical — decomposable into pure frequencies via the Fourier transform. And it is accessible — translatable into visual patterns that convey the same information through a different sense.

This studio explores all three dimensions simultaneously. Every tool is built from first principles: a custom FFT implementation, real-time Web Audio processing, and Canvas-based generative visualization — all running locally in your browser, no servers, no data collection.
