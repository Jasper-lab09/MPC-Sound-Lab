§ 01

Research Question

Can we understand, generate and reinterpret sound through mathematics?

Direction A

Analyze sound

Decompose live audio into waveforms, spectra and harmonic structures using custom FFT and real-time signal processing.

Direction B

Generate music

Translate mathematical sequences and geometric rhythms into audible patterns — from Fibonacci to Euclidean algorithms.

Direction C

Understand physical phenomena

Connect pressure waves, cochlear mechanics and room acoustics back to the equations that describe them.

Direction D

Make sound accessible

Build cross-modal translations so sound information can be experienced visually and understood without hearing.

Project Philosophy

Mathematics, physics, music and accessibility are not separate disciplines here. They are lenses on the same phenomenon: vibration becoming information.

Built from first principles. Every tool starts with the equations — the Fourier transform, the wave equation, the harmonic series — and turns them into interactive instruments.

Honest about limitations. dBFS is not dB SPL. Bin spacing is not frequency resolution. Visual translation is not hearing. We say so clearly.

Local and private. All processing happens in the browser. No audio leaves your device. No accounts, no tracking, no cloud dependencies.

Open by default. The code is open source. The experiments are reproducible. The findings are documented, even when they are unremarkable.
