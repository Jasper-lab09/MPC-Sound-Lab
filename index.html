Interactive Scientific Laboratory

Where sound becomes data,
data becomes music,
and music becomes accessible.

MPC Sound Lab is a browser-based platform for exploring the intersection of physics, mathematics, and audio engineering. Analyze live sound, generate music from mathematical structures, and experience audio through visual translation — all in real time, right here in your browser.
