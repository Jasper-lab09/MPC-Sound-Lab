02 Mathematical Foundation
Fibonacci Rhythms

The Fibonacci sequence (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, ...) can be mapped to rhythmic patterns by taking values modulo the pattern length:

F(n) mod M → step position

This creates naturally evolving, non-repetitive but structured sequences. The golden ratio φ = (1+√5)/2 ≈ 1.618 emerges as the limit ratio F(n+1)/F(n), and patterns based on it avoid exact repetition while remaining coherent.

Euclidean Rhythms

The Euclidean algorithm for GCD can distribute n hits as evenly as possible across m steps:

E(n, m) → [x . . x . . x .]

This produces traditional world rhythms:

E(3, 8) → tresillo: [x . . x . . x .]
E(5, 8) → cinquillo: [x . x . x . x .]
E(2, 3) → habanera pattern

Harmonic Series & Just Intonation

For a fundamental frequency f₀, harmonics are fn = n · f₀. The ratios between early harmonics produce the consonant intervals of just intonation:

Interval ratios
Octave: 2:1
Fifth: 3:2
Fourth: 4:3
Major 3rd: 5:4
Minor 3rd: 6:5
Equal temperament
f = 440 · 2(n-69)/12

where n is the MIDI note number. A4 = 440 Hz is the reference.

03 Physical Foundation

Consonance and the harmonic series. When a vibrating string or column of air produces a note, it does not vibrate at a single frequency. The physical modes of vibration produce a series of integer-multiple overtones. The ear's cochlea physically separates frequencies along its tapered basilar membrane, and harmonics that align with integer ratios create regular excitation patterns that the auditory system processes as "consonant."

Why Euclidean rhythms sound good. Even distribution of hits (the Euclidean pattern) maximizes the minimum gap between hits. This regularity maps onto the brain's beat-tracking circuitry, which predicts the timing of the next event. When predictions are confirmed, the reward circuit engages — the rhythm "feels right."

Web Audio synthesis. The OscillatorNode generates a waveform at a specified frequency. By connecting it to a GainNode (envelope) and scheduling note on/off events, we can play mathematical patterns as audible music. The AudioContext clock (sample-accurate) ensures tight timing.
